# Beta-Test Notes

## From CS person

### Things done well
- Player movement and shooting feels responsive
- Controls are intuitive
- Shop is cool
- Lots of enemy variety

### Things that could be improved
- Make it more clear that you need to use number keys to upgrade
- Show max health in center alongside regular health
- Easy to clump enemies up
- Maybe assets that aren't just cirlces
- More movement such as a dash to make it more interesting

## From not-CS person

### Things that could be improved

- Gameplay was boring

- Visuals are crappy
